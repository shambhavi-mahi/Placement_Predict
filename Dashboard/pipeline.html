🛡️ Why Pipelines Prevent Leakage

When you call pipeline.fit(X_train, y_train), every step inside the pipeline is fitted exclusively on the training data. The scaler learns the training mean and standard deviation — it never sees validation or test values.

At inference time, pipeline.predict(X_val) applies the already-fitted transformers before passing data to the model. No information leaks across the split boundary.

# ✅ Safe — leakage impossible
pipe = Pipeline([
  ('scaler', StandardScaler()),
  ('model', LogisticRegression())
])

pipe.fit(X_train, y_train)
# scaler fitted on X_train only

preds = pipe.predict(X_val)
# scaler.transform applied automatically
