Key Mathematical Difference: L1 vs L2 vs MedAE

MSE/RMSE (L2): Squares residuals, so single huge outliers disproportionately drag the regression line.
MAE (L1): Penalizes linear distances, significantly less sensitive to extremes.
MedAE: Takes the median of errors, completely ignoring extreme outlier tails.
