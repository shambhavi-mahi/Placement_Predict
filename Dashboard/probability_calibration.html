Why Brier Score Matters: The Brier Score measures the mean squared error between predicted probabilities and actual 0/1 outcomes. A lower score reflects higher probabilistic accuracy.

Comparing Calibration Algorithms

Why Random Forests Are Uncalibrated: Averaging decision tree votes rarely outputs 0.0 or 1.0 because extreme variance pushes probabilities toward the sample prior mean (the "bagging bias").
Platt Scaling (Sigmoid): Fits a logistic regression model $P(y=1|f) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp(Af + B)}$ over validation scores. Excellent for small datasets (<1,000 samples) where non-parametric approaches might overfit.
Isotonic Regression: Fits a non-parametric, monotonic step-function. Can correct arbitrary distortion shapes without assuming a sigmoid curve. Highly recommended for datasets with ≥1,000 samples.
