⚠️ MDI Bias

MDI (built-in feature importance) can overrate high-cardinality features because trees get more opportunities to split on them. For more reliable estimates, use Permutation Importance on the validation set.

How Permutation Importance Works

A feature's column is randomly shuffled on the validation set, breaking its relationship with the target. The drop in accuracy = how much the model relied on that feature. This is model-agnostic and avoids MDI's high-cardinality bias.

What SHAP Adds

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) gives per-sample explanations — for any individual student, you can see exactly how much each feature pushed the prediction toward "Placed" or "Not Placed". MDI and Permutation Importance only give global averages.
