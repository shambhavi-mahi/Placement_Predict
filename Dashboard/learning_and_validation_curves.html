Diagnostic Curves

📖 Reading the Learning Curve

  • High Bias (Underfitting): Train score is low and Validation score plateaus early at a similar low number. Adding more data will NOT help. (Solution: More complex model, more features).
  • High Variance (Overfitting): Huge gap between high train score and lower validation score. Validation score continues creeping upward. (Solution: Add more data, apply regularizer/pruning).

📖 Reading the Validation Curve

  • Left side (Low Depth): Underfitting zone where model lacks capacity to capture relationships. Both train and validation scores are mediocre.
  • Sweet spot (Depth = {{ data.optimal_depth }}): Validation score peaks. Optimal balance between bias and variance.
  • Right side (High Depth): Overfitting zone. Train accuracy approaches 100%, but validation accuracy drops off due to memorization.
