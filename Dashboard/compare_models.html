📖 How to Read This Table

  • Val Acc — accuracy on held-out data. This is what matters in production.
  • F1 — balances precision & recall. Better than accuracy for imbalanced classes.
  • AUC — area under the ROC curve. 1.0 = perfect, 0.5 = random guess.
  • Overfit Gap — Train Acc minus Val Acc. Large gap = model memorized training data.
