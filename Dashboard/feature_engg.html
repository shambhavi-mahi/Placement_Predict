📊 Standard Scaler (Z-Score)

Centres each feature to mean = 0 and scales to std = 1. Best for algorithms that assume normally distributed data (logistic regression, SVM, PCA). Sensitive to outliers.

xscaled = x − μ σ

🛡️ Robust Scaler

Uses median as centre and IQR (Q3 − Q1) as scale. Outliers have much less influence compared to Min-Max or Standard scaling. Ideal when the dataset contains extreme values.

xscaled = x − median IQR (Q3 − Q1)
