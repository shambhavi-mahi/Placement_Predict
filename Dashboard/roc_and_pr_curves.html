ROC vs PR: When to Use Which?

ROC-AUC: Measures ranking quality across both positive and negative classes equally. Insensitive to class prevalence. Good when both classes are equally important.
PR-AUC: Focuses strictly on True Positives vs False Positives. Highly recommended when the positive class is rare or when false alarms carry severe business penalties.
