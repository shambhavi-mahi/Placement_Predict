Why Modern Boosting?

🛡️

Regularization (XGBoost)

L1 + L2 penalties on leaf weights reduce overfitting — something sklearn GB lacks natively.

🎲

Column Subsampling

Random feature selection per tree (like Random Forest) reduces correlation between boosted trees.

🍃

Leaf-wise Growth (LightGBM)

Grows the leaf with the highest loss reduction rather than level-by-level, enabling faster convergence.
