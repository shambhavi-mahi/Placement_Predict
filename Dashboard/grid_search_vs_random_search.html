Why Random Search Dominates in Practice

Bergstra & Bengio (2012) proved that in high-dimensional spaces, only a few hyperparameters have significant impact. Grid search wastes time exploring uninformative parameter variations, whereas Random search covers distinct points along the important dimensions in fewer iterations.
