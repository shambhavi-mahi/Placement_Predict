K-Means vs Hierarchical — When to Use Which?

K-Means
  • Requires specifying K upfront.
  • Fast — scales to millions of rows.
  • Assumes spherical, similarly-sized clusters.
  • Sensitive to feature scale → always standardise.
  • Use Elbow / Silhouette to pick K.
Hierarchical
  • Builds a full tree — cut at any level post-hoc.
  • No need to fix K before fitting.
  • Dendrogram reveals multi-level structure.
  • Memory-intensive — subsampling needed for large N.
  • Ward linkage usually gives compact clusters.
