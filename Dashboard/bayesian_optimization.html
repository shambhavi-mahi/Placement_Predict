How Bayesian Optimization Works

1. Gaussian Process (Surrogate Model): Fits a smooth probabilistic prior over the objective function, outputting both a predicted performance ($\mu$) and epistemic uncertainty ($\sigma$).
2. Acquisition Function (UCB): Combines $\mu(x) + \kappa \cdot \sigma(x)$. It queries points where predicted mean is high (Exploitation) OR where uncertainty is high (Exploration).
3. Sample Efficiency: Requires drastically fewer expensive model evaluations than Grid or Random Search to find near-optimal configurations.
